Attendance Biometric System

Secure fingerprint-based attendance tracking

How to Use

1. Enter your unique biometric token in the User Token field.
2. For entry, optionally provide an item token.
3. Click "Record Entry" to mark your arrival.
4. Click "Record Exit" to mark your departure.
